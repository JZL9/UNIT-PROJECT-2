01 — The Vision

A Nation Building Its Future

Saudi Vision 2030 was launched in 2016 by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman as a roadmap to transform the Kingdom.

Its goal is to diversify the economy beyond oil, open the country to the world, and improve the quality of life for every citizen. At the center of this plan are giga-projects: entire cities, destinations and communities built from the ground up to show what the future of Saudi Arabia can look like.
