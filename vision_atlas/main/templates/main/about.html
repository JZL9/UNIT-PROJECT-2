Saudi Vision 2030

The roadmap transforming the Kingdom's economy, society and future.

01 — Overview

What Is Vision 2030?

Saudi Vision 2030 was announced in April 2016 by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman as a long-term plan to build a stronger and more diverse Saudi Arabia.

It builds on the Kingdom's three strengths: its position at the heart of the Arab and Islamic worlds, its power as a global investment leader, and its location as a hub connecting Asia, Europe and Africa. The vision is delivered through national programs that cover housing, health, tourism, culture, industry and more.

02 — The Pillars

Three Pillars

A Vibrant Society

A society proud of its Islamic values and national identity, with better quality of life through culture, sports, entertainment and healthy living.

A Thriving Economy

An economy less dependent on oil, with growing private sector, small businesses, tourism and industry, creating new jobs for young Saudis.

An Ambitious Nation

An effective and transparent government, and responsible citizens who contribute to the nation through work and volunteering.
