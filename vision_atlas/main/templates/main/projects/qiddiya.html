Chapter 01

The Capital of Entertainment

Qiddiya was announced in 2017 as a new city dedicated to entertainment, sports and culture. Built on the edge of the Tuwaiq cliffs, just outside Riyadh, it is developed by Qiddiya Investment Company, owned by the Public Investment Fund.

Chapter 02

Rides That Break Records

At its heart is Six Flags Qiddiya City, a theme park with rides designed to set world records for speed and height, including a roller coaster that launches riders off the edge of the cliff.

Chapter 03

A Water Park in the Desert

Aquarabia brings huge slides, wave pools and family attractions to the heart of the desert, offering a cool escape for visitors all year round.

Chapter 04

Sports, Speed and Gaming

Qiddiya also plans a motorsport track built into the cliffs, a major stadium overlooking the valley, and a district dedicated to gaming and esports, making it a home for fans of every kind.
