Chapter 01

Homes for a New Generation

ROSHN was launched in 2020 by the Public Investment Fund as a national real estate developer. Its mission supports a key Vision 2030 goal: helping more Saudi families own their homes.

Chapter 02

SEDRA, the First Community

SEDRA in north Riyadh was ROSHN's first community, built around green spaces, family homes and easy access to schools, mosques and shops.

Chapter 03

Designed for Everyday Life

Every ROSHN neighborhood is planned to be walkable, with parks, sports areas and daily services close to home, so families spend less time in cars and more time together.

Chapter 04

Living by the Water

In Jeddah, ROSHN is building coastal communities like ALAROUS and MARAFY, where homes sit along waterfronts and canals that bring the sea into the neighborhood.

Chapter 05

Beyond Real Estate

ROSHN's name is now part of Saudi sports too. Since 2023 it has been the title sponsor of the Saudi Pro League, known today as the ROSHN Saudi League.
