A Vision Born in 2017

NEOM was announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman at the Future Investment Initiative in Riyadh in 2017, backed by the Public Investment Fund. The name combines the Greek word "neo", meaning new, with "M" from the Arabic word "Mustaqbal", meaning future. The goal is to build a new model for living, working and doing business, powered by renewable energy.

Where Sea, Mountains and Desert Meet

NEOM sits in the far northwest of the Kingdom, where the Red Sea meets the Gulf of Aqaba. Within one region you find coral coastlines, golden deserts and mountain peaks that see snow in winter. This mix of landscapes gives NEOM a milder climate than much of the region and makes it a destination for year-round tourism.

Thousands of Years of History

Long before NEOM, this land was a crossroads for ancient trade routes linking Arabia with the Levant and Egypt. Thousands of rock inscriptions and carvings have been found across the area, giving a rare window into the languages and daily life of early Arabian civilizations.

Designed Around Nature

NEOM plans to keep the vast majority of its land as protected nature. The NEOM Nature Reserve focuses on bringing back wildlife, replanting native vegetation and protecting the coral reefs along its coast, so that development and nature can grow side by side.

A New Economy

Beyond tourism, NEOM is built to attract new industries: renewable energy, green hydrogen, water, advanced manufacturing, mobility and technology. Oxagon, its port city, is planned as a hub for clean industry and global trade on one of the world's busiest shipping routes.
