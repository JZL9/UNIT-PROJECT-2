Chapter 01

A Vision Born in 2017

NEOM was announced by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman at the Future Investment Initiative in Riyadh in 2017, backed by the Public Investment Fund. The name combines the Greek word "neo", meaning new, with "M" from the Arabic word "Mustaqbal", meaning future.

Chapter 02

Where Sea, Mountains and Desert Meet

NEOM sits in the far northwest of the Kingdom, where the Red Sea meets the Gulf of Aqaba. Within one region you find coral coastlines, golden deserts and mountain peaks that see snow in winter.

Chapter 03

Thousands of Years of History

Long before NEOM, this land was a crossroads for ancient trade routes linking Arabia with the Levant and Egypt. Thousands of rock inscriptions have been found across the area.

Chapter 04

Designed Around Nature

NEOM plans to keep the vast majority of its land as protected nature, bringing back wildlife, replanting native vegetation and protecting the coral reefs along its coast.

Chapter 05

A New Economy

Beyond tourism, NEOM is built for new industries: renewable energy, green hydrogen, water, advanced manufacturing and technology. Oxagon, its port city, is planned as a hub for clean industry and global trade.
