Chapter 01

A New Kind of Destination

The Red Sea project was announced in 2017 and is developed by Red Sea Global, a company owned by the Public Investment Fund. Its idea is simple but ambitious: luxury tourism that gives back to nature instead of taking from it.

Chapter 02

More Than 90 Islands

The destination stretches along Saudi Arabia's west coast and includes a huge lagoon with more than 90 untouched islands, along with desert dunes, dormant volcanoes and mountain canyons.

Chapter 03

Regenerative Tourism

Only a small part of the site is being developed, and most of the islands are left completely natural. Millions of mangrove trees are being planted along the coast to protect wildlife and capture carbon.

Chapter 04

One of the World's Richest Reefs

The coast is home to a large coral reef system known for its resilience to rising sea temperatures. It shelters turtles, dolphins and hundreds of fish species, making it a dream for divers and snorkelers.

Chapter 05

Open to the World

In 2023 the first resorts welcomed guests and Red Sea International Airport began operations. The whole destination is designed to run on renewable energy from the sun.
