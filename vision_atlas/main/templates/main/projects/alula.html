Chapter 01

A Crossroads of Civilizations

For thousands of years, AlUla sat on the ancient Incense Route, where caravans carried frankincense and spices across Arabia. The Dadanite, Lihyanite and Nabataean kingdoms all left their mark here.

Chapter 02

Hegra, Carved in Stone

Hegra holds more than a hundred monumental tombs carved into sandstone by the Nabataeans. In 2008 it became Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Chapter 03

A Desert of Wonders

Giant rock formations like Elephant Rock, deep canyons and wide valleys make AlUla feel like an open-air gallery. Even modern buildings like Maraya, the mirrored concert hall, reflect the landscape around them.

Chapter 04

Life in the Oasis

A green oasis of palm and citrus trees runs through the valley. Beside it stands AlUla Old Town, a village of mud-brick houses that was home to families for centuries.

Chapter 05

Protecting Nature

The Royal Commission for AlUla, founded in 2017, is protecting the region's wildlife through nature reserves like Sharaan, including a program to bring back the endangered Arabian leopard.
